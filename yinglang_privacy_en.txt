1. Information we process
When you choose Phone gallery, Stillglide requests photo read access and reads the URI, file name, and image content of photos you permit it to access. On Android 14 and later, you may grant access to selected photos only. When you choose a folder, Android's system picker grants access to that folder. We use this access to display its images and, where supported, request that Android move an image to trash.

Favorites, selected source, reflection and sound preferences, privacy acceptance, and language are stored on your device. Favorites are identified by local image URI. The app attempts to update Android's IS_FAVORITE flag, but a manufacturer's Gallery may not display it, so Stillglide has its own favorites page. Notes in the debug list build are stored locally. Stillglide has no account feature and does not upload your original photos.

The current release build has ads disabled and does not include Umeng or Bugly. A future release configured for Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) may process advertising identifiers, device and network information, IP address, and ad interactions after applicable consent. This policy and store disclosures must be updated for the exact published build.

When the vault is enabled, the app reads the image, creates and verifies an encrypted copy in app-private no-backup storage, and stores its name and optional note locally. The encryption key is kept in Android Keystore. The vault has no cloud sync or separate PIN or biometric lock. After Android confirms deletion, the original is removed from the system gallery. Restoration writes a new gallery image. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes the encrypted files and key, making vault photos unrecoverable.

2. Purposes, retention, and trash
Photos are processed on your device for browsing, favorites, and requests to move them to Android's system trash. Local favorites and settings remain until you clear app data or uninstall the app. Without optional media management access, Android normally asks you to confirm each trash operation. On Android 12 and later, granting this special access can remove the per-photo confirmation. After a successful operation, the photo disappears from the active gallery. Android controls how long trashed photos remain and when they are deleted. Settings > Recently deleted lists trashed photos that Android permits this app to access and lets you request restoration. Some device makers' Gallery apps use a separate Recently Deleted area, so an item in Android MediaStore trash may not appear there. Photos from folders that cannot be mapped to Android's media library are kept and are not permanently deleted by Stillglide.

Vault copies in builds that enable the feature remain until you restore them, clear app data, or uninstall. Before a move, the app prominently warns that the original disappears from the system gallery, may not appear in Recently deleted, and that needed vault photos must be restored or separately backed up before uninstalling. The encrypted copy is removed only after a successful restore.

3. Permissions
Photo read permission supports Phone gallery. Folder access supports a folder you choose. If you deny broad photo access, you can still select a folder through Android's picker. On Android 12 and later, optional media management special access is enabled only after you open Android settings from the app and grant it; it removes repeated confirmation when moving accessible photos into or out of trash. You can revoke it in Android settings. Network permission is used only by the advertising module when configured.

4. Third parties and transfers
The current release build has ads disabled and includes no Umeng or Bugly SDK. Before enabling AdMob/UMP, the operator must list its provider, data, purpose, privacy-policy link, any cross-border transfer, and available controls. Stillglide does not intentionally send original photos to ad SDKs. SDK behavior must be checked in the exact published build.

5. Your choices
You can revoke photo or folder access in Android settings. If ads are enabled, UMP supplies applicable ad privacy options. Clearing app data removes local favorites, notes, vault files and their encryption key, but does not clear favorite flags already written to Android's media library or restore media already removed by Android.

6. Children, changes, and contact
Stillglide is intended for general audiences. Minors should use trash controls with a parent or guardian. We will notify users in the app of material policy changes. For questions about local records or this policy, contact the email above.
