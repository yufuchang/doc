1. Information we process
When you choose Phone gallery, Stillglide requests photo read access and reads the URI, file name, and image content of photos you permit it to access. On Android 14 and later, you may grant access to selected photos only. When you choose a folder, Android's system picker grants access to that folder. We use this access to display its images and, where supported, request that Android move an image to trash.

For images accessible through Android MediaStore, Stillglide reads and updates the system IS_FAVORITE flag; that flag is the source of truth when the app refreshes. Favorites for folder images that cannot be mapped to MediaStore, the selected source, reflection and sound preferences, privacy acceptance, and language are stored on your device. Some manufacturers' Gallery apps and cloud photo services maintain separate favorite systems and may not mirror Android's IS_FAVORITE flag. Notes in the debug list build are stored locally. Stillglide has no account feature and does not upload your original photos.

This release uses Google AdMob app open and banner ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for applicable privacy choices. After you accept this policy, UMP checks whether a consent message is required. The Google Mobile Ads SDK is initialized and ads are requested only when UMP reports that ads may be requested. Google may process your IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, advertising ID, app set ID, and other applicable device or account identifiers for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention. Consent choices, regional rules, and device settings can affect which identifiers and ads are used. Stillglide does not send original photos to the ad SDK.

When the vault is enabled, the app reads the image, creates and verifies an encrypted copy in app-private no-backup storage, and stores its name and optional note locally. The encryption key is kept in Android Keystore. The vault has no cloud sync or separate PIN or biometric lock. After Android confirms deletion, the original is removed from the system gallery. Restoration writes a new gallery image. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes the encrypted files and key, making vault photos unrecoverable.

2. Purposes, retention, and trash
Photos are processed on your device for browsing, favorites, and requests to move them to Android's system trash. Local folder favorites and settings remain until you clear app data or uninstall the app; MediaStore favorite flags remain under Android's control. Without optional media management access, Android normally asks you to confirm each trash operation. On Android 12 and later, granting this special access can remove the per-photo confirmation. After a successful operation, the photo disappears from the active gallery. Android controls how long trashed photos remain and when they are deleted. Settings > Recently deleted lists trashed photos that Android permits this app to access and lets you request restoration. Some device makers' Gallery apps use a separate Recently Deleted area, so an item in Android MediaStore trash may not appear there. Photos from folders that cannot be mapped to Android's media library are kept and are not permanently deleted by Stillglide.

Vault copies in builds that enable the feature remain until you restore them, clear app data, or uninstall. Before a move, the app prominently warns that the original disappears from the system gallery, may not appear in Recently deleted, and that needed vault photos must be restored or separately backed up before uninstalling. The encrypted copy is removed only after a successful restore.

3. Permissions
Photo read permission supports Phone gallery. Folder access supports a folder you choose. If you deny broad photo access, you can still select a folder through Android's picker. On Android 12 and later, optional media management special access is enabled only after you open Android settings from the app and grant it; it removes repeated confirmation when moving accessible photos into or out of trash. You can revoke it in Android settings. Network permission supports UMP and AdMob. The app has no photo-upload service.

4. Third parties and transfers
Google LLC provides AdMob and UMP. Google's SDK may automatically collect and share the data listed above with Google to deliver and measure ads, manage consent, and prevent fraud. Google says Google Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit with TLS. Google may process data outside your country or region. See Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy and Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure at https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure. We do not use other advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs in this release.

5. Your choices
You can revoke photo or folder access in Android settings. Where required, UMP presents a consent message; the Ad privacy options entry in Settings lets you revisit available choices. You may also reset or delete the Android advertising ID in device settings. Clearing app data removes local folder favorites, settings, consent state, notes, vault files and their encryption key, but does not clear favorite flags already written to Android's media library or restore media already removed by Android. Google handles its retained advertising data under its own policy; contact us at the email above for help with app data.

6. Children, changes, and contact
Stillglide is intended for general audiences. Minors should use trash controls with a parent or guardian. We will notify users in the app of material policy changes. For questions about local records or this policy, contact the email above.
